Which air fryer to buy in 2026 without wasting money

Two models that genuinely sell well in Brazil. The math isn't "the most expensive is the best" — it's family size, counter space and how much you'll actually use it.

Prices below are a reference from August 2026, cross-checked with Mercado Livre listings. The value shown on the button may have changed — the store's checkout is the final source.

1. Best value for money — Britânia BFR50

5.5-liter basket and 1,500 W. It's the sweet spot: fits potatoes, chicken and vegetables for 3 or 4 people without going to the oven-style format, which takes over the whole kitchen. On Amazon it holds a 4.8 rating; the recurring praise is practicality and easy cleaning. The recurring gripe is the light-duty finish — it's not an appliance meant to be passed down.

Get this one if the criterion is "works every day and doesn't hurt your wallet."

View Britânia BFR50

2. Best compact — Electrolux EAF15

3.2 liters, 1,400 W, 8 programs. Starting at R$ 269,90 on Mercado Livre. Quiet, fast and easy to store. If you live alone or in a studio, you don't need a family-size basket — it'll just mean more dishes to wash for no reason. The reported weak point is the basket's non-stick coating after several washes.

How we decided

Sales volume, a high minimum rating, brand reputation and spec sheet weighed against the asking price. We did not test the appliances in a lab: we used public marketplace data. If the store's listing differs from the spec sheet, the listing takes precedence.
