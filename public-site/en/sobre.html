Editorial

How we choose what makes it into the showcase

Achado Agora isn't a store. It's a curation desk: fewer SKUs, more criteria. The click only exists if the value-for-money math adds up.

What we look at

  • Sales volume and average rating on the marketplace.
  • Asking price against the category's recent average.
  • Spec sheet versus what the listing promises.
  • Brand and seller reputation (Full fulfillment, invoicing, obvious complaints).

What we don't do

  • We don't automatically redirect to checkout.
  • We don't make up stock, shipping or flash discounts.

Reference price

Every card shows a value checked on a given date. Marketplaces change prices all the time. The button opens the store so you can see the real number, the seller and the timeline.
