How it works

From listing to checkout, no hidden shortcuts.

1

We select

Only products with strong sales volume, high ratings and prices that make sense make the cut — editorial criteria, not guesswork.

2

You decide here

Spec sheet, pros, cons and reference value. If it's not right for you, there's no magic button pushing the purchase.

3

The store closes the sale

The click opens Mercado Livre, Amazon or Shopee. Shipping, stock and final price always come from there.

Questions

Before you click

Do you sell the product?

No. We're an editorial showcase. The purchase happens on the marketplace, under the store's own exchange policy.

Is the price shown on the site the final price?

No. It's a reference checked on the date shown for each deal. Coupons, shipping and stock can change at checkout.

Why does the button say "view deal"?

Because the destination is the store where the purchase is completed — the click just takes you there.
